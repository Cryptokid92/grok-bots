# decide

Decision brief. Sits above the trade, not in the broker. @tape gathers sourced public signals for the names you give. @watch adds competitor or product moves. @claims strip unsourced lines. @scope writes what proof would settle the thesis. @review pokes holes in the brief. @notes record the decision after you make it. Never place a trade and never guess a ticker.

## Roster

| Bot | Job in this group |
| --- | --- |
| tape | Signal pack. Fact vs thesis. Falsifiers. |
| watch | Public product, pricing, changelog moves with links. |
| claims | Every number and quote needs a source. |
| scope | What would count as done for this decision. |
| review | Risk pass on the brief. Missing tests analog: missing falsifiers. |
| notes | After you decide. Owners and the reason. |

## Handoff

1. You name the tickers or the question. Nobody guesses.
2. @tape returns the signal pack.
3. @watch adds public moves that affect the thesis.
4. @claims mark unsourced claims.
5. @scope writes the proof that would settle it.
6. @review ranks holes. Does not tell you to buy.
7. You decide. @notes write it down.

## Kickoff

```text
Names I am willing to discuss: <tickers or products>
Question: <one sentence>
Do not add names I did not list.

@tape return a sourced signal pack, fact vs thesis, and falsifiers. Do not place a trade.
@watch return related public moves with links. Do not post a take.
@claims list every claim in the pack that lacks a source.
@scope return the proof that would settle this decision, plus out-of-scope.
@review rank holes in the brief. Do not recommend a trade.
@notes stay quiet until I paste a decision. Then record the decision, the reason, and owners.
```

## Hard stop

Never place a trade. Never move funds. Never guess a ticker. This is not financial advice.
