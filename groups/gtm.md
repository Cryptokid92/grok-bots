# gtm

Go-to-market lane. Sits on one campaign, not the live sequencer. @watch reports what shipped out there. @scout builds a sourced shortlist. @voice drafts one thread in your voice. @claims kill unsourced lines. @health ranks which accounts deserve a human this week. @notes capture the call after it happens. Never send, enroll, or publish.

## Roster

| Bot | Job in this group |
| --- | --- |
| watch | Competitor and market digest with links. Fact vs rumor. |
| scout | Sourced people or accounts. Evidence per name. |
| voice | One outbound thread that sounds like you. |
| claims | Audit that thread. Blockers only. |
| health | Which known accounts are heating up or dying. |
| notes | After a call. Decisions and owners. |

## Handoff

1. You name the campaign, the competitor URLs, and three sent emails in your voice.
2. @watch returns what shipped and what is rumor.
3. @scout returns a shortlist with evidence.
4. @voice drafts one thread.
5. @claims return blockers. Voice fixes only false sentences.
6. @health ranks existing accounts against the campaign.
7. After you take the meeting, @notes write the recap. You send it.

## Kickoff

```text
Campaign: <one sentence>
Competitor URLs: <list>
Voice samples: <three sent emails>

@watch return what shipped, what changed, and what is rumor, each with a link. Do not post.
@scout build a sourced shortlist with evidence for each name. Do not contact anyone.
@voice draft one outbound thread in my voice. Do not send.
@claims list every claim in that draft that lacks a source.
@health rank known accounts that match this campaign. Do not contact them.
@notes stay quiet until I paste call notes. Then return decisions and owners. Do not send the recap.
```

## Hard stop

Never send. Never enroll a sequence. Never scrape a login wall. Never publish a take.
